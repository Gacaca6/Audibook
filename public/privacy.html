Privacy Policy

Audibook · Last updated 18 July 2026

The short version: Audibook does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Everything you add to Audibook stays on your own device.

Information we collect

None. Audibook has no user accounts and no backend server that stores user data. We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage analytics.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, using your browser's storage on your own device. It is never uploaded anywhere, and it is deleted when you remove a book or uninstall the app:

  • Books you add, including chapter text extracted from your own EPUB, PDF, or TXT files
  • Audiobook chapters you choose to download for offline listening
  • Cover artwork for books on your shelf
  • Your progress: XP, streak, completed quizzes, and your chosen narrator voice

Files you open or share into Audibook are processed entirely on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Network connections

Audibook connects to the internet for exactly two purposes:

  • archive.org — to search the free LibriVox audiobook collection, and to stream or download the recordings and cover art you select.
  • archive.org (Project Gutenberg mirror) — to fetch the matching public-domain book text used to generate comprehension quizzes.

These requests contain only the search terms and the item you chose. No personal information is attached. Their operator, the Internet Archive, has its own privacy policy at archive.org/about/terms.

Permissions

Audibook requests storage persistence so your downloaded audiobooks are not evicted by the browser. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Background activity and notifications

If a chapter download is interrupted because you lost connection, Audibook asks your browser to finish it in the background once you are online again. That retry re-requests the same audio file from archive.org and saves it on your device — nothing is sent anywhere.

Audibook can show local notifications, for example to tell you a queued download finished. These are generated on your own device only, and only if you have already allowed notifications. Audibook operates no server and maintains no push subscription list, so it cannot and does not send you marketing messages.

Children's privacy

Audibook is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children, and because the app collects no data at all, it collects nothing from anyone of any age.

Content

Audiobooks offered in the app are public-domain recordings produced by LibriVox volunteers and hosted by the Internet Archive. Book text used for quizzes comes from Project Gutenberg, also public domain. Any book you add yourself remains entirely private to your device.

Data deletion

Because nothing leaves your device, you are always in full control. Removing a book from your shelf deletes it and its downloaded audio immediately. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser settings, erases everything permanently. There is no server-side copy for us to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
